AI Visibility shows how often and how prominently the active brand appears across its tracked buyer questions.

## Read the score responsibly

The score summarizes results from the current tracked prompt cohort and provider set. Use the underlying prompts, mentions, citations, and comparison period to understand why the score moved.

## Competitor comparisons

Competitor results use the competitors configured for the brand. A competitor mention is not an owned-brand mention, and total citations must not be presented as the brand's citations.

## Run analysis

Run Analysis refreshes AI visibility results for the tracked prompts and supported providers. It does not refresh Google Analytics or Search Console.

## Use LanternIQ

Ask LanternIQ to explain a movement, identify weak prompts, compare owned and competitor citation pressure, or recommend the next measurable action.
