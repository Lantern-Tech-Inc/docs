The AI Visibility page is your brand's analytics hub for AI-powered search. It shows how often your brand appears when buyers ask questions relevant to your category across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini.

## AI Visibility score

Your **AI Visibility Score** is a 0–100 measure of how prominently your brand appears in AI-generated responses.

The score is calculated by submitting buying-intent prompts to multiple AI models and measuring whether your brand is mentioned, how prominently, and in what context.

**Score card shows:**
- Current score (0–100)
- Delta vs the previous period
- Trend chart over time

## AI Rankings table

Below the score chart, the rankings table shows:

- **Your brand's rank** — where you appear in AI-generated responses vs competitors
- **Competitor comparison** — how your score compares against brands you track
- **Rank delta** — whether you moved up or down since the last analysis

## Top Sources

The **Top Sources** section shows which third-party domains AI models cite most often when answering questions about your category.

Use this to identify which publications, review sites, and platforms have the most influence over AI responses — and whether your brand has coverage there.

<Tip>
  If your score is rising but AI referral traffic isn't, it suggests AI models mention you but don't link to you — a content and structured data problem worth investigating with the [Citations](/analytics/citations) page.
</Tip>

## Running an analysis

Click **Run Analysis** to trigger a fresh visibility scan. Results populate all four tabs: Visibility, Traffic, Citations, and Search Console.
