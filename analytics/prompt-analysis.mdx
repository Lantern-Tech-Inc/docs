The Prompt Analysis page has two sections: **Analysis** and **Prompt Library**.

## Analysis

Shows AI response patterns when your tracked prompts are submitted to AI models. You can see:

- Which prompts your brand appears in
- Which prompts you're absent from
- Patterns in how AI models answer questions in your category

## Prompt Library

The Prompt Library is a collection of buying-intent questions that Lantern submits to AI models during visibility analysis runs.

**Managing topics:**

1. Click **Add Topic** to create a topic group (e.g. "Project Management", "Content Marketing").
2. Add individual prompts under each topic (e.g. "What is the best content marketing tool for SMBs?").
3. Lantern uses these prompts in every future visibility analysis run.

**Editing and deleting:**

- Click the pencil icon next to any topic or prompt to rename it inline.
- Click the trash icon and confirm to delete.

<Note>
  The quality of your prompt library directly determines the accuracy of your AI Visibility score. Well-written buying-intent prompts that reflect how your actual buyers search produce actionable data. Aim for 20–30 prompts covering your category broadly — "best [category] tools", "how to [problem you solve]", and "[your brand] alternatives".
</Note>
