Citations shows the sources referenced in tracked AI answers.

## Source categories

- Owned: pages on the active brand's domains.
- Competitor: pages associated with configured competitors.
- Independent: publications, communities, directories, and other external sources.

Keep these categories separate when comparing performance.

## Use citation evidence

Look for owned pages that already earn citations, competitor pages influencing answers, and independent sources that shape category trust.

A citation opportunity is a prioritization signal, not a guarantee that outreach or new content will produce a citation.
