title: "Traffic"
description: "Review website activity from a connected Google Analytics property, including AI referrals."

Traffic shows website activity from the connected Google Analytics property, with emphasis on referrals from AI assistants and answer engines.

## Requirements

Connect Google Analytics and select the correct property. Data availability and attribution depend on the source values recorded by that property.

## What to review

- Total sessions and movement over the selected period.
- AI referral sessions.
- Landing pages.
- Acquisition sources.
- Geographic and engagement context where available.

AI referral classifications are based on recorded referral information. They do not capture every influence that occurred before a visit.
