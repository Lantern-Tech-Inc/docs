## Why Heading Structure Matters

Proper heading hierarchy helps AI platforms understand your content's organization, main topics, and relationships between sections. It's one of the easiest and most impactful on-page SEO improvements you can make.

<Info>
  **AI Insight**: AI platforms like ChatGPT and Perplexity use heading structure to extract key information and determine what to cite from your content.
</Info>

## The Heading Hierarchy

### H1 - Main Page Title
- ✅ Only ONE H1 per page
- ✅ Should match or closely mirror your title tag
- ✅ Describes the main topic of the page
- ✅ 20-70 characters optimal

```html
<h1>AI Search Analytics Platform for Marketing Teams</h1>
```

### H2 - Main Sections
- ✅ Multiple H2s allowed
- ✅ Breaks content into major topics
- ✅ Should form a logical outline

```html
<h2>Real-time Brand Monitoring</h2>
<h2>Attribution Tracking</h2>
<h2>Citation Analysis</h2>
```

### H3 - Subsections
- ✅ Child topics under H2
- ✅ Provides additional detail

```html
<h2>Real-time Brand Monitoring</h2>
  <h3>Track ChatGPT Mentions</h3>
  <h3>Monitor Perplexity Results</h3>
  <h3>Analyze Gemini Responses</h3>
```

### H4-H6 - Deeper Levels
- ✅ Use sparingly
- ✅ For complex, detailed content
- ✅ Maintain logical hierarchy

## Proper Heading Hierarchy Example

```html
<article>
  <!-- Main Title -->
  <h1>Complete Guide to AI Search Optimization</h1>
  
  <!-- Introduction (optional) -->
  <p>Learn how to optimize your content for AI platforms...</p>
  
  <!-- Major Section 1 -->
  <h2>Understanding AI Search</h2>
  <p>AI search refers to...</p>
  
    <!-- Subsection 1.1 -->
    <h3>What is AI Search?</h3>
    <p>Content about AI search basics...</p>
    
    <!-- Subsection 1.2 -->
    <h3>How AI Search Differs from Traditional SEO</h3>
    <p>Key differences include...</p>
    
      <!-- Sub-subsection 1.2.1 -->
      <h4>Crawling and Indexing</h4>
      <p>AI platforms crawl differently...</p>
      
      <!-- Sub-subsection 1.2.2 -->
      <h4>Ranking Factors</h4>
      <p>AI prioritizes different signals...</p>
  
  <!-- Major Section 2 -->
  <h2>Technical Optimization</h2>
  <p>Technical aspects of AI search...</p>
  
    <h3>Structured Data</h3>
    <p>Schema markup helps AI...</p>
    
    <h3>Meta Tags</h3>
    <p>Essential meta tags include...</p>
  
  <!-- Major Section 3 -->
  <h2>Content Strategy</h2>
  <p>Creating AI-friendly content...</p>
</article>
```

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="1" title="One H1 per page">
    Use exactly one H1 tag that describes the page's main topic:
    
    ```html
    <!-- Good -->
    <h1>Lantern: AI Search Analytics Platform</h1>
    
    <!-- Bad: Multiple H1s -->
    <h1>Welcome</h1>
    <h1>Our Product</h1>
    <h1>Get Started</h1>
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="list-ol" title="Don't skip levels">
    Maintain hierarchical order (don't jump from H2 to H4):
    
    ```html
    <!-- Good -->
    <h2>Main Topic</h2>
      <h3>Subtopic</h3>
        <h4>Detail</h4>
    
    <!-- Bad: Skipping H3 -->
    <h2>Main Topic</h2>
      <h4>Detail</h4>
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="text" title="Use descriptive text">
    Make headings clear and descriptive:
    
    ```html
    <!-- Good -->
    <h2>How to Track Brand Mentions in ChatGPT</h2>
    
    <!-- Bad -->
    <h2>Introduction</h2>
    <h2>Next Steps</h2>
    <h2>Learn More</h2>
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="key" title="Include keywords naturally">
    Use target keywords in headings when natural:
    
    ```html
    <!-- Good -->
    <h1>AI Search Analytics: Track Brand Visibility Across AI Platforms</h1>
    <h2>Monitor ChatGPT and Perplexity Brand Mentions</h2>
    
    <!-- Bad: Keyword stuffing -->
    <h1>AI Search Analytics AI Search Tool AI Platform AI Tracking</h1>
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="ban" title="Don't use headings for styling">
    Never use headings just to make text bigger:
    
    ```html
    <!-- Bad: Using H3 for styling -->
    <h3>Click here for more info</h3>
    
    <!-- Good: Use CSS for styling -->
    <p class="large-text">Click here for more info</p>
    ```
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Testing Your Heading Structure

<Steps>
  <Step title="View as outline">
    Look at just your headings - do they form a logical outline?
    
    ```
    H1: AI Search Analytics Platform
      H2: Features
        H3: Real-time Monitoring
        H3: Attribution Tracking
      H2: Pricing
        H3: Starter Plan
        H3: Pro Plan
    ```
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Check hierarchy">
    Ensure no levels are skipped (H2 → H3 → H4, not H2 → H4)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Count H1s">
    Verify you have exactly one H1 per page
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Use browser tools">
    Browser extensions like HeadingsMap can visualize structure
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Ask AI">
    Paste your content into ChatGPT and ask "What are the main topics?"
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common Mistakes and Fixes

| Mistake | Problem | Fix |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Multiple H1s | Confuses AI about main topic | Use only one H1 |
| Skipping levels | Breaks hierarchy logic | Use sequential levels |
| Generic headings | Not descriptive enough | Make specific and clear |
| All H2s | No content hierarchy | Use H3s for subsections |
| Headings for style | Semantic confusion | Use CSS for styling |
| No keywords | Missing SEO opportunity | Include target keywords naturally |
| Too long | Hard to scan | Keep concise (5-10 words) |

## Heading Length Guidelines

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="1" title="H1: 20-70 characters">
    ```html
    <!-- Good -->
    <h1>AI Search Analytics Platform for Marketers</h1>
    
    <!-- Too short -->
    <h1>Analytics</h1>
    
    <!-- Too long -->
    <h1>The Complete AI Search Analytics Platform for Marketing Teams Who Want to Track Brand Visibility</h1>
    ```
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion icon="2" title="H2: 20-60 characters">
    ```html
    <!-- Good -->
    <h2>Real-time Brand Monitoring</h2>
    
    <!-- Good -->
    <h2>How to Track ChatGPT Mentions</h2>
    ```
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion icon="3" title="H3-H6: Flexible">
    Can be shorter or longer based on content needs
    
    ```html
    <h3>Installation</h3>
    <h3>Configure Your Brand Profile Settings</h3>
    ```
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Accessibility Considerations

Proper heading structure is essential for accessibility:

```html
<!-- Good: Clear hierarchy for screen readers -->
<h1>Getting Started Guide</h1>
  <h2>Step 1: Installation</h2>
    <h3>Download the Package</h3>
    <h3>Install Dependencies</h3>
  <h2>Step 2: Configuration</h2>
    <h3>Basic Settings</h3>
    <h3>Advanced Options</h3>
```

<Tip>
  Use ARIA labels when needed:
  ```html
  <h2 aria-label="Section about pricing plans">💰 Pricing</h2>
  ```
</Tip>

## Heading Structure Checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="One H1">
    ✅ Exactly one H1 per page
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Logical hierarchy">
    ✅ No skipped levels (H2 → H3 → H4)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Descriptive text">
    ✅ Clear, specific heading text
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Keywords included">
    ✅ Natural keyword usage in headings
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Proper nesting">
    ✅ H3s under H2s, H4s under H3s
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Scannable outline">
    ✅ Headings form logical outline
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Don't overdo it**: While headings are important, don't force them. Use them naturally to organize your content.
</Warning>
