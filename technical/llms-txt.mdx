## What is llms.txt?

`llms.txt` is an emerging standard file that provides structured information specifically for Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI platforms. Think of it as a "README for AI" that helps AI understand and cite your content correctly.

<Info>
  **New Standard**: llms.txt is becoming the standard way to communicate with AI platforms like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Gemini.
</Info>

## Why llms.txt Matters

Traditional files like robots.txt and sitemaps were designed for traditional search engines. AI platforms need different information:

- ✅ Clear descriptions of what your site/company does
- ✅ Key facts and data points to cite
- ✅ Preferred ways to describe your brand
- ✅ Links to authoritative content
- ✅ Structured data for accurate responses

## Where to Place llms.txt

Place your `llms.txt` file at your domain root:

```
https://asklantern.com/llms.txt
```

Also reference it in your robots.txt:

```txt
# AI Platform Information
# See our llms.txt for detailed information for LLMs
# https://asklantern.com/llms.txt

User-agent: GPTBot
Allow: /
```

## Format Guidelines

- ✅ Plain text format (.txt)
- ✅ UTF-8 encoding
- ✅ Use # for section headers
- ✅ Use ## for subsections
- ✅ Keep lines under 80 characters when possible
- ✅ Use clear, structured formatting
- ❌ No HTML or Markdown formatting
- ❌ No special characters or emojis
- ❌ No promotional language or hype

<Warning>
  **Keep it truthful**: AI platforms prioritize accuracy. Don't include misleading or exaggerated claims.
</Warning>
