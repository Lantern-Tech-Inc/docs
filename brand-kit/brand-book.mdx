The Brand Book is where you create and manage the brands in your workspace. Each brand stores all the context Lantern needs to produce accurate, on-brand outputs.

## What a brand stores

- **Name** and **Website URL**
- **Description** — used as context for every AI run involving this brand
- **Voice and tone guidelines**
- **Competitor list** — domains Lantern tracks for gap analysis and citation monitoring
- **Topics** — keyword focus areas that inform content planning

## Creating a brand

1. Click **New Brand**
2. Enter the brand name, website URL, and optional description
3. Click **Create**

## Editing a brand

Click into any brand card to edit all fields inline. Changes apply to all future agent runs for that brand.

## Deleting a brand

Click `⋯` → **Delete**. All associated data — visibility scores, citations, and SEO data — is permanently removed.

<Note>
  Most analytics pages default to your first brand. If you manage multiple brands, use the brand selector at the top of each page to switch context.
</Note>
