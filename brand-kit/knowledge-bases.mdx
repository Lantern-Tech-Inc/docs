title: "Knowledge bases"
description: "Add approved sources that give authorized Lantern work reliable brand context."

Knowledge bases give agents approved internal sources to retrieve when public web evidence is not enough.

## Supported sources

Add webpages or upload PDF, DOCX, and TXT files.

## Processing states

- Pending: waiting to begin.
- Processing: content is being extracted and prepared.
- Ready: available for retrieval.
- Failed: processing did not complete.

## How retrieval works

Lantern divides sources into searchable passages and retrieves relevant context for an authorized agent request. A knowledge base does not force every passage into every request.

## Good source hygiene

Use current product documentation, approved claims, brand guidance, research, and case studies. Remove obsolete sources and avoid uploading credentials or secrets.
