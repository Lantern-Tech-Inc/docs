title: "Content and CMS drafts"
description: "Review imported content, generated drafts, refresh workflows, and connected CMS behavior."

Content availability depends on the connected CMS and the workflows available to your workspace.

## Imported content

When supported by the connected CMS, Lantern can show existing content for review and planning. Availability depends on the connection, account access, and CMS configuration.

## Generated drafts

Specialists can produce content drafts and related artifacts, such as briefs, metadata, and FAQs. Review each output before moving it into an external publishing workflow.

## Refresh workflows

Use a refresh workflow to assess an existing page, identify opportunities, and prepare a revised draft. The result does not automatically publish changes.

## Publishing

Publishing behavior depends on the CMS connector, workflow configuration, permissions, and approval boundary. Contentful uses a draft-first workflow. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview) for connector guidance.
