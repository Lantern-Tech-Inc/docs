The Content page shows blog posts from your connected CMS. HubSpot is fully supported; WordPress, Sanity, Contentful, and Webflow are on the roadmap.

## Content table

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Title | Post title with a link to the live post |
| Status | Draft, Published, or Scheduled |
| Published date | When the post went live |
| Last updated | Most recent edit timestamp |

## Refreshing content

Each row has a **Refresh** action. Click it to trigger an agent that:

1. Fetches the current content of the post
2. Checks what competitors are now ranking for the same keyword
3. Identifies outdated statistics, missing subtopics, and structural issues
4. Rewrites or augments the post to bring it up to date
5. Publishes the refreshed version back to your CMS

<Tip>
  Refreshing existing content is one of the most effective ways to improve AI citations. AI models favour content that is authoritative, comprehensive, and recently updated.
</Tip>

## Connecting HubSpot

If HubSpot is not yet connected, the page shows a connection prompt.

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations**
2. Click **Connect HubSpot**
3. Complete the OAuth flow — the Content page populates automatically

See [Integrations](/settings/integrations) for full setup details.
