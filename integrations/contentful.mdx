Connect Contentful to create structured content drafts from approved Lantern workflows.

## Before you connect

Choose the Contentful space, environment, locale, and content type used for drafts. Confirm the field mapping for title, slug, body, excerpt, SEO metadata, publication date, and tags where those fields exist.

## Draft-first behavior

The current Lantern Contentful adapter creates drafts and rejects live publication. Review and publish the entry from Contentful using your normal editorial process.

## Required access

The connection uses Contentful content-management authorization. Use an account permitted to manage the intended space and environment.

## Troubleshooting

If a draft cannot be created, verify the space, environment, locale, content type, required fields, field types, and connection authorization. Reconnect when the authorization has expired or been revoked.
