## What is Lantern?

Lantern is the full-stack marketing agent platform built for the AI era. Think of it as the operating system for your marketing team — describe what you want, and a multi-agent system plans and executes it step by step in real time.

The pipeline is genuinely end-to-end: keyword discovery from Google Search Console, People Also Ask, and Reddit → SERP and competitor gap analysis → structured content brief → writing → voice QA → CMS publish. Beyond blog content, Lantern handles press releases, journalist pitches, newsletters, presentations, and Google Ads campaign copy.

On top of content, Lantern monitors your brand visibility across every major AI engine — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google — so you always know where you stand and what to fix.

<Info>
  **1 billion+ people** search with AI every day. If your brand isn't cited, you're invisible. Lantern keeps you visible and handles the work to get you there.
