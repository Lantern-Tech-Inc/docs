## What is Lantern?

Lantern is the marketing agent platform built for modern marketing teams. Track how your brand is represented across every major search engine and AI engine — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google — then deploy autonomous agents that close citation gaps, create content, and drive measurable revenue. All without the manual overhead.

<Info>
  **1 billion+ people** search with AI every day. If your brand isn't included, you're invisible. Lantern Agents keep you visible without the operational overhead.
</Info>
