## Lantern documentation

Lantern is a governed AI marketing platform that connects brand context, AI-search intelligence, marketing decisions, and specialist execution.

Use Lantern to understand what matters, decide what to do next, create reviewable work, and measure what changed.

## Start with LanternIQ

LanternIQ is the conversational intelligence workspace on Home. Ask a question about your brand, visibility, traffic, search performance, content, competitors, or current work.

LanternIQ can answer from approved brand context and connected data, investigate a question with evidence, recommend a measurable next action, prepare work for review, and start bounded specialist work when you request a deliverable.

LanternIQ does not publish, spend, send outreach, change integrations, or make other consequential external changes directly.

## Recommended first steps

1. Complete onboarding and review the generated brand context.
2. Connect Search Console and Google Analytics when available.
3. Review the initial AI visibility baseline and prompt library.
4. Ask LanternIQ what deserves attention first.
5. Review the evidence and approve or refine the proposed work.
