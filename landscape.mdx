The market has split into four distinct categories. Most tools solve one part of the problem well. The gap between insight and execution is where most teams are losing.

## AI search monitoring

Tools in this category track where your brand appears — or doesn't — across AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

Profound · Otterly · Scrunch · Peec · Promptwatch · Evertune

**Where Lantern is different:** Monitoring tools tell you the gap exists. Lantern closes it. When an agent surfaces a missing citation, it generates the fix, publishes it to your CMS, and connects the result to traffic and revenue — without your team assembling a separate content workstream.

---

## AI content creation

Tools in this category help teams produce marketing content at scale using AI.

Jasper · Writesonic

**Where Lantern is different:** Content tools help you write. They do not tell you whether your brand is winning inside AI answers, and they do not connect that content to AI search outcomes. Lantern generates content specifically to close citation gaps and then measures whether it worked.

---

## AI marketing operations

Tools in this category automate marketing workflows through configurable AI pipelines.

AirOps · Goodie by NoGood · Bear AI

**Where Lantern is different:** Workflow tools give you a workbench — you configure and assemble the pipeline. Lantern deploys a system. Agents monitor, research, write, and publish from day one without canvas setup or workflow assembly.

## Enterprise SEO

Conductor

**Where Lantern is different:** Conductor is built around traditional search. Lantern is built for the shift to AI search, with monitoring, content execution, and attribution designed around citation presence — not just keyword rankings.

## The operating loop most tools are missing

Every category above solves a piece. What they don't solve together is the loop:

| Step | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Monitor** | Track citation presence across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google in real time |
| **Detect** | Identify exactly which prompts you're absent from and why |
| **Execute** | Deploy agents that research the gap, write the fix, and publish to your CMS automatically |
| **Attribute** | Connect citation gains to visits and conversions via script or CDN integration |

Lantern is the only platform that runs this loop end-to-end without switching tools.
