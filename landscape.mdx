The AI search and content marketing space has grown quickly. Most tools solve one part of the problem — monitoring, writing, or workflow automation. Lantern is built to do all three in a single orchestrated platform.

## AI search monitoring

These tools track brand and competitor visibility across AI engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.

| Company | Focus |
|---|---|
| [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com) | AI answer monitoring and brand visibility analytics |
| [Otterly](https://otterly.ai) | GEO visibility tracking across AI search engines |
| [Scrunch](https://scrunch.com) | AI search presence and citation monitoring |
| [Peec](https://peec.ai) | AI search rankings and share-of-voice tracking |
| [Promptwatch](https://promptwatch.com) | Prompt-level AI response monitoring and analytics |
| [Evertune](https://evertune.ai) | AI brand sentiment and visibility tracking |

**Where Lantern fits:** Lantern includes AI visibility monitoring as part of a broader platform. The difference is that when Lantern surfaces a gap — a missing citation, a competitor outranking you in AI responses — it can immediately run an agent to fix it. Monitoring tools stop at the insight.

## AI content creation

These tools generate marketing content using AI.

| Company | Focus |
|---|---|
| [Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai) | AI writing assistant for marketing copy and long-form content |
| [Writesonic](https://writesonic.com) | AI content generation across blog posts, ads, and landing pages |

**Where Lantern fits:** Lantern writes content too, but the pipeline starts earlier (keyword research, GSC data, competitor gap analysis, content brief) and ends later (voice QA, CMS publish). Content agents are one part of a broader workflow, not a standalone writing tool.

| Company | Focus |

| [Goodie by NoGood](https://higoodie.com) | GEO and AI search optimisation platform from growth agency NoGood |

**Where Lantern fits:** The closest category to Lantern. The distinction is depth of the SEO and GEO intelligence layer — Lantern agents are grounded in live data from Google Search Console, DataForSEO, People Also Ask, and Reddit, and publish directly to your CMS with a human-in-the-loop review step.
