The Home page has three areas: the AI chat, quick action cards, and your recent agents list.

## AI chat

The large input at the top is a multi-turn AI assistant. Describe what you want in plain English and Lantern handles the rest.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe your goal">
    Type what you want — "Write a blog post on generative engine optimisation" or "Run an SEO report for acme.com".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer any clarifying questions">
    The assistant may ask one or two focused questions to sharpen the goal (e.g. target audience, website URL).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    It proposes what it's about to do in one sentence and asks: "Ready to go?" — confirm to launch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track progress">
    A link to the running agent appears in the chat so you can follow progress in real time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Suggestion chips**

If your brand is configured, pre-filled suggestion chips appear below the input:

- Write a blog post for [Brand]
- Run an SEO report for [Brand website]
- Draft a press release for [Brand]
- Create a Google Ad campaign
- What can you help me with?

Click any chip to pre-fill and immediately submit it.

<Tip>
  The Home chat is the fastest path from idea to running workflow. Use it for one-off requests. Use the [Agents](/agents) page to build workflows that run on a schedule.
</Tip>

## Recent agents

The bottom of Home shows your six most recently updated agents with their last run time. Click any to open the agent detail page.
