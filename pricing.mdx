## AI visibility

Free includes ChatGPT visibility monitoring. Pro, Agency, and Enterprise include tracking across ChatGPT, Claude, Google, and Gemini.

Each Agency client workspace includes the same Pro-level execution limits: 100 agent runs per month, Beam batches of up to 100 cells, available integrations, unlimited brand knowledge bases, and ChatGPT Ads.

## Support

Priority support is included with Pro, Agency, and Enterprise. Enterprise includes email, dedicated Slack, and a dedicated specialist with a 24-hour SLA.

## Trial

Trials include one brand, one seat, 50 agent runs for the trial period, and Beam batches of up to 10 cells. Integrations, knowledge bases, ChatGPT Ads, single sign-on, and priority support are not included in a trial.

## Understanding usage

An agent run is one accepted specialist-agent execution. Continuation turns and infrastructure retries do not use another included run. A customer-triggered retry uses another run.

Beam batch size is the maximum number of cells that can be selected and run in a single Beam batch.

Seats include current members and outstanding invitations. Included agent runs are tracked against the current billing period.

## Agency add-ons

Agency includes three client workspaces and five agency seats. Each client workspace receives its own Pro-level execution allowance. Add-ons expand workspace or seat capacity only. They do not increase the per-workspace agent-run or Beam limits.

| Add-on | Price | Billing | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Extra client workspace | $299 | Per month, per workspace | Adds one client workspace |
| Extra agency seat | $29 | Per month, per seat | Adds one agency seat |

Your monthly Agency total is the base price plus any selected client-workspace and seat add-ons.
