## Understanding usage

An agent run is one accepted specialist-agent execution. Continuation turns and infrastructure retries do not use another included run. A customer-triggered retry uses another run.

Concurrent runs are active agent executions shared across your organization. Work above the concurrent-run limit stays queued until capacity is available.

Beam batch size is the maximum number of cells that can be selected and run in a single Beam batch.

Seats include current members and outstanding invitations. Included agent runs are tracked against the current billing period.

## Agency add-ons

Agency includes three client workspaces and five agency seats. Add-ons expand workspace or seat capacity only. They do not increase included agent runs, concurrent runs, or Beam batch size.

| Add-on | Price | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Extra client workspace | $299/month | Adds one client workspace |
| Extra agency seat | $29/month | Adds one agency seat |

Your monthly Agency total is the base price plus any selected client-workspace and seat add-ons.
