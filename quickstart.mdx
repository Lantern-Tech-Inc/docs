## Get Started in Three Steps

Start monitoring your brand mentions across AI platforms and optimize your content strategy.

### Step 1: Create Your Account

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="user-plus" title="Sign up for Lantern">
    Visit [asklantern.com](https://asklantern.com) and create your free account. No credit card required for your 7-day trial.
    
    <Tip>Take advantage of our New Year offer: Get 50% off for 3 months!</Tip>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="building" title="Set up your brand profile">
    Complete your brand profile to help Lantern track the right data:
    
    1. **Company name and website URL** - Your primary brand identity
    2. **Add your key products or services** - Lantern will track mentions of specific products
    3. **Define your main competitors (optional)** - Benchmark your visibility against competitors
    4. **Select the AI platforms you want to monitor** - Choose from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Claude
    
    <Info>Lantern will start tracking your brand mentions immediately after setup.</Info>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 2: Configure Monitoring

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="magnifying-glass" title="Add prompts to track">
    Define the queries you want to monitor across AI platforms:
    
    - Product comparison queries: "What are the best [product category]?"
    - Direct brand queries: "Tell me about [your brand]"
    - Use case queries: "Best solution for [specific need]"
    - Competitive queries: "Compare [your brand] vs [competitor]"
    
    <Tip>Start with 5-10 high-priority prompts and expand from there.</Tip>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="link" title="Install tracking code">
    Add Lantern's tracking snippet to your website to monitor AI-generated traffic:

    ```html
    <script>
      (function(l,a,n,t,e,r,n){
        // Lantern tracking code will be provided in your dashboard
      })();
    </script>
    ```

    Place this code in your website's `<head>` section to start tracking visitors from AI conversations.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 3: Start Optimizing

<Accordion icon="rocket" title="Review your insights">
  1. Check your real-time monitoring dashboard
  2. Review which AI platforms mention your brand
  3. Identify citation sources and content gaps
  4. Create or optimize content based on insights
  5. Track improvements over time
</Accordion>
