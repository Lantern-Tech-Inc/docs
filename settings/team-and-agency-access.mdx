Lantern supports organization roles and client-brand access.

## Organization roles

- Owner: controls the organization and highest-level administration.
- Admin: manages organization work and supported settings.
- Member: works within the access granted by the organization.

## Client brand roles

- Client admin.
- Client editor.
- Client viewer.

Client roles scope access to the relevant brand workspace. Invitations expire after seven days and count against seat limits when accepted.

## Agency workspaces

Agency users can switch between authorized client brands. Keep client data, conversations, Projects, integrations, and approvals in the correct brand context.
