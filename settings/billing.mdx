---
title: "Billing"
description: "View and manage your Lantern subscription."
---

Go to **Settings → Billing** to manage your plan, payment method, and invoices.

## What you can do

- See your current plan and usage (agent runs remaining, seats used)
- Upgrade or downgrade your plan
- Update your payment method
- Download past invoices
- Cancel your subscription

Billing is handled via Stripe. Click **Manage Billing** to open the Stripe customer portal in a new tab.

## Plan limits

Your current agent run count and seat usage are shown at the top of the page. If you're near a limit, an upgrade prompt appears. Contact [support](mailto:collins@asklantern.com) if you have questions about your plan.
