The SEO Overview shows a high-level summary of your brand's organic search performance. Data is populated by running an SEO audit agent.

## Key metrics

- Domain authority and trust signals
- Top ranking pages and their keyword positions
- Pages with declining traffic (content refresh candidates)
- Technical health summary

## Running your first SEO audit

If no data exists yet, an empty state prompts you to run your first audit.

1. Click **Run SEO Audit** or go to the [Home chat](/home) and type "Run an SEO audit for [your website]".
2. The agent crawls your site and populates all SEO pages with real data.
3. Return to the Overview for a summary, or go to [Site Audit](/seo/site-audit) for the full issue list.
